\subsection*{Conclusion}

Pull-back geodesics capture the true structure of the learned manifold better than straight-line Euclidean distances, curving along the data rather than cutting through empty space. However, a single VAE is unreliable -- different training runs recover different geometries. A small ensemble of decoders fixes this: by penalising paths where models disagree, the geometry stabilises, with a lot of the gain already achieved at just two decoders.
